The Area

All About Mandeville, LA

Mandeville is a Northshore city located in St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana, along the northern edge of Lake Pontchartrain. The area is known for its residential communities, lakefront access, and relaxed pace compared to nearby urban centers. Mandeville combines suburban living with outdoor amenities while remaining connected to the greater Southeast Louisiana region.

The city has grown steadily as a place for families, professionals, and outdoor enthusiasts who value access to green spaces, local businesses, and regional transportation routes.

Interesting Facts About Mandeville

  • Northshore Location: Mandeville is part of Louisiana's Northshore region, known for residential development and lakefront communities.
  • Lake Pontchartrain Access: Proximity to the lake supports boating, fishing, and outdoor recreation.
  • Tammany Trace Trail: A non-motorized transportation route that links several Northshore cities and supports biking and walking.
  • Community Events: Seasonal markets, local festivals, and community gatherings take place throughout the year.

Things To Do in Mandeville

  • Fontainebleau State Park: A large state park offering walking and biking trails, camping facilities, picnic areas, and lake access. It is commonly used for outdoor recreation and nature-based activities.
  • Mandeville Lakefront: A scenic area along Lake Pontchartrain featuring walking paths, open green spaces, playgrounds, and views of the lake. The lakefront is a popular spot for exercise and casual gatherings.
  • Mandeville Trailhead: A central community location that hosts local markets, festivals, and events. It also serves as a trail access point for the Tammany Trace.
  • Tammany Trace: A multi-use paved trail that connects several Northshore communities. It is frequently used for biking, walking, and recreational travel between nearby cities.

Notable Residents of Mandeville

  • Bubby Brister: Former NFL quarterback associated with Louisiana's Northshore region.
  • Pat Brister: Business professional with ties to the local community.
  • Joseph Bulovas: Collegiate football placekicker with connections to the Mandeville area.

Public Transportation in Mandeville

  • St. Tammany Area Transit (STAR): Provides limited public transportation services within St. Tammany Parish.
  • Rideshare Services: Uber and Lyft operate throughout Mandeville and nearby areas.
  • Lake Pontchartrain Causeway: A direct roadway connection between the Northshore and the South Shore of Lake Pontchartrain.
  • Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport: Located south of the lake, offering access to domestic and international flights.
