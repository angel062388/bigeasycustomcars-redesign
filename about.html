Our Story

Born in a Garage in New Orleans

Big Easy Custom Cars started with a small garage, a few tools, and a love for custom rides. Born in New Orleans, we grew from weekend builds with friends to full-scale custom work trusted across the Gulf South.

We're builders first. Not salespeople, not parts pushers. Our crew has real hands-on experience, folks who grew up wrenching, painting, tuning, and chasing the next great build. That's what drives us. We don't follow trends. We create builds that reflect the people behind the wheel.

What makes us different is simple, we care about the craft. From the way a truck sits to how it sounds pulling out the lot, we think about every detail. We mix Southern grit with big city style and let the results speak for themselves.

Our shop has become a home base for people who want more than a stock ride. Some come to turn heads. Others come to hit the trails. All leave with something real, something built, not bought.

We're proud of where we come from. New Orleans taught us to work hard, build bold, and always put style into the details.

This is what we do. This is who we are. Big Easy Custom Cars.
